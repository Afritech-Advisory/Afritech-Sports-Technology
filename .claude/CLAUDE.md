# Afritech Sports: working rule for this repo

Before writing or changing any page, card, dashboard or text in this repo, read
the Afritech Four Sentences (the afritech-four-sentences skill and the
afritech-four-sentences memory file):

1. Who this is for
2. What they get
3. Why they'd believe us
4. The one action we want them to take

Every page's first screen must pass the stranger test: someone outside football
can say who it's for and what to do next. If they would ask "so what do you
actually do?", rewrite before publishing.

When work here shows a sharper way to say one of the four sentences for this
repo's reader, put it to Andrew as a lettered Gatekeeper question. Don't change
the master yourself.
